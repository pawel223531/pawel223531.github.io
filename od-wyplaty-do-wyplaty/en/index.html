Android app

From Payday to Payday

A simple way to plan your budget, expenses, and upcoming bills between paydays.

What you can do

  • Set your payday and standard income amount.
  • Plan one-time and recurring expenses.
  • Add income adjustments to the current payday.
  • Review your cycle calendar and forecast available funds.

Data and privacy

Budget information entered in the app is stored locally on your device. The app uses Google Mobile Ads to display ads; the SDK provider may process technical data described in the privacy policy.
